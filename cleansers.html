SKIN SCIENCE · LESSON 02

CLEAN
SERS

A simple introduction to the science of cleansing, surfactants, and how cleansers interact with the skin.

01 · THE FOUNDATION

Start with
cleansing.

Cleansing is one of the most basic parts of skincare. A cleanser helps remove substances from the surface of the skin, including excess oil, sweat, dirt, and some products.

Understanding how cleansing works can help you make more informed choices about skincare instead of choosing products based only on marketing claims.

02 · CLEANSER SCIENCE

How does a
cleanser work?

Many cleansers contain ingredients called surfactants. Surfactants can help water interact with oily substances, allowing substances on the skin to be lifted away during rinsing.

THE SCIENCE

Water + oil.

Water and oil do not naturally mix well. Some cleanser ingredients help bridge this difference, allowing oily substances and other material on the skin to be removed more easily with water.

The goal of cleansing is not to remove every trace of oil from the skin. The skin naturally produces sebum, which is part of the skin's normal protective environment.

03 · CLEANSER TYPES

Different
formulas.

Cleansers come in many forms. The texture and ingredients can differ depending on the formula and its intended use.

01

Gel

Gel cleansers are often lightweight and may contain surfactants designed to remove surface oils and other substances.

02

Cream

Cream cleansers are often formulated with a richer texture and may be designed to cleanse while helping reduce the feeling of dryness.

03

Foaming

Foaming cleansers create a lather. The amount of foam does not automatically determine how effective a cleanser is.

04

Oil

Oil-based cleansers can help dissolve and remove oil-soluble substances, including some makeup and sunscreen.

04 · KEY IDEA

Clean skin
isn't stripped skin.

A cleanser does not need to leave your skin feeling extremely tight or dry to be effective. Cleansing should remove unwanted substances while respecting the skin's natural barrier.

How a cleanser feels can vary from person to person, so skincare choices should be based on your individual skin and how it responds.
