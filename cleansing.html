Lesson 02 · Skincare

Cleansing

Learn how cleansing works, why it matters, and how different cleansers interact with the skin.

01 — Learn

Understanding cleansing.

Cleansing helps remove substances from the skin's surface while supporting everyday skin health.

01

What is cleansing?

Cleansing removes substances such as sweat, excess oil, dirt, and other materials from the skin's surface.

02

How cleansers work

Many cleansers contain surfactants that help lift oils and particles so they can be rinsed away with water.

03

Types of cleansers

Cleansers can have different formulas and textures, including gels, creams, foams, and other formulations.

04

The skin barrier

A good cleansing routine should support the skin barrier rather than unnecessarily irritating or drying the skin.
