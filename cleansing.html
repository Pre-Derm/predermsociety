SKIN SCIENCE · LESSON 02

CLEANSING

A simple introduction to cleansing and the science behind keeping the skin's barrier healthy.

01 · THE FOUNDATION

Clean skin.
Healthy barrier.

Cleansing helps remove substances that build up on the skin, including sweat, excess oil, environmental particles, and products.

The goal is not to make your skin feel "squeaky clean." A good cleansing routine should clean the skin while helping maintain its natural protective barrier.

02 · CLEANSING SCIENCE

What does
cleansing do?

Cleansers are designed to help remove substances from the surface of the skin. Different formulas can work differently depending on their ingredients and how they interact with oil and water.

THE BIG IDEA

More cleansing
isn't always better.

Cleansing too aggressively or too frequently can interfere with the skin barrier and may leave skin feeling dry, tight, or irritated.

Dermatology is not about making skin as "clean" as possible. It is about supporting the skin's normal functions.

03 · UNDERSTANDING CLEANSERS

Different
formulas.

Cleansers come in many forms. The best choice can depend on a person's skin, preferences, and how their skin responds to the product.

01

Gel

Gel cleansers are often lightweight and can provide a fresh cleansing feel. Formula ingredients matter more than the label alone.

02

Cream

Cream cleansers can contain ingredients that help make cleansing feel less drying and may be useful for people who prefer a more moisturizing texture.

03

Oil

Oil-based cleansers can help dissolve oil-soluble substances such as makeup and sunscreen before the skin is rinsed.

04 · KEY IDEA

The goal is
balance.

Healthy skin does not need to feel extremely dry after cleansing. A gentle routine aims to remove unwanted buildup while respecting the skin barrier.
