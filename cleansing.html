Cleansing

Learn what cleansing does, why it matters, and how to think about cleansers from a scientific perspective.

01 — Learn

The science of cleansing.

01

What cleansing does

Cleansing helps remove substances from the skin's surface, including sweat, excess oil, dirt and some products.

02

How cleansers work

Many cleansers contain surfactants. These ingredients help loosen oily and water-based substances so they can be rinsed away.

03

Too much cleansing

Very frequent or harsh cleansing can disturb the skin barrier and may leave skin feeling dry, tight or irritated.

04

Gentle habits

A simple routine is often enough: use a suitable cleanser, rinse thoroughly and avoid aggressive scrubbing.
