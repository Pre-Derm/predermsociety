About Us

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led space for exploring dermatology, medicine, skincare, research, and healthcare.

Our Story

Curiosity can become a career.

Pre-Derm Society was created by students who wanted to make dermatology and medicine easier to explore at a young age.

We believe students should be able to ask questions, discover science, learn about healthcare, and explore the many paths within medicine.

Our goal is to create a welcoming student community centered around curiosity, education, and the science of skin.

Our Mission

Learn. Research. Discover medicine.

We want to make dermatology, skincare science, and healthcare education accessible and exciting for students.

What We Do

A place to explore medicine.

Pre-Derm Society combines education, research, service, and student creativity.

01

Education

We create student-friendly resources that make dermatology and medical science easier to understand.

02

Research

We encourage students to ask questions, explore science, and become curious about the research behind medicine.

03

Service

We hope to use what we learn to help spread health education and support communities around us.
