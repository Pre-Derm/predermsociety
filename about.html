Pre-Derm Society is a student-led space created to make dermatology, medicine, skincare, research, and healthcare more approachable for students.

01 — Who We Are

Learning medicine
starts with asking why.

Pre-Derm Society was created as a student-led community for people who are curious about dermatology, medicine, science, skincare, and healthcare.

Our goal is to make medical and scientific ideas easier to explore through student-friendly lessons, research resources, educational projects, and opportunities.

We believe you don't have to already know everything about medicine to start being curious about it.
