A student-led space created to make dermatology, medicine, research, skincare, and healthcare more accessible to students.

01 · OUR STORY

Medicine starts
with curiosity.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led community focused on exploring dermatology and the science behind skin health.

We create educational resources, introduce students to research, share opportunities, and encourage young people to ask questions about medicine and healthcare.

03 · OUR MISSION

Make medicine
feel possible.

We believe students should be able to explore medicine before they ever have to decide what they want their future to look like.

Through lessons, research resources, opportunities, and student-led education, Pre-Derm Society turns curiosity into action.

04 · OUR IMPACT

Every lesson completed and every student reached helps us build a stronger community of future researchers, healthcare professionals, and curious learners.
