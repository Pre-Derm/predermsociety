ABOUT PRE-DERM SOCIETY

Why we exist.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led organization designed to make medicine, science, dermatology, research, and healthcare opportunities easier for students to discover.


We want students to be able to start with an interest — like dermatology, pediatrics, pharmacy, or research — and actually find somewhere to go next.


Our goal is to make information feel organized, approachable, and accessible so that students can explore medicine before they ever have to decide exactly where they belong.
