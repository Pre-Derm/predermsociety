01 · OUR MISSION

Making medicine
easier to explore.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led educational initiative created to make dermatology, skin science, and medicine easier to understand and explore.

We believe students should be able to discover opportunities, learn from reliable educational resources, and explore careers without needing to know where to start.

Our goal is to learn, teach, and give back by creating resources that students can actually use.
