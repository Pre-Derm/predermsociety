About
Pre-Derm Society.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led space for exploring dermatology, medicine, skincare, research, and healthcare.

01 — Who We Are

Learn.
Explore.
Make an impact.

Pre-Derm Society was created to give students a place to learn about dermatology and medicine in a simple, accessible, and student-friendly way.

Through educational lessons, research resources, medical opportunities, and student projects, we encourage curiosity and evidence-based learning.

Our goal is to make exploring medicine feel exciting, approachable, and meaningful.
