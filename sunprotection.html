Lesson 04 · Skin Health

Sun Protection

Learn about ultraviolet radiation, sunscreen, SPF, and everyday strategies for protecting skin.

01 — Learn

Understanding UV exposure.

01

UVA

UVA radiation reaches deeper into the skin and contributes to long-term skin changes and photoaging.

02

UVB

UVB is strongly associated with sunburn and can damage skin cells.

03

SPF

SPF describes a sunscreen's ability to protect primarily against UVB-related sunburn when tested under standardized conditions.

04

More than sunscreen

Shade, protective clothing, hats and avoiding intense midday sunlight can also reduce UV exposure.
