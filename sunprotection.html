SKIN SCIENCE · LESSON 05

SUN
PROTECTION

A simple introduction to UV radiation, sunscreen, and why protecting your skin matters throughout your life.

01 · THE FOUNDATION

Protect your
skin.

Sunlight contains ultraviolet radiation, commonly called UV radiation. Too much UV exposure can damage skin cells and contribute to premature skin aging and skin cancer.

Sun protection is about reducing unnecessary UV exposure and protecting your skin whenever you are outdoors.

02 · UV RADIATION

UVA.
UVB.

UVA and UVB are two types of ultraviolet radiation that reach Earth's surface. They affect the skin in different ways.

UVA

UVA

UVA radiation penetrates deeper into the skin and contributes to skin aging and long-term skin damage.



UVB

UVB

UVB radiation is strongly associated with sunburn and can damage the DNA of skin cells.

03 · KEY IDEA

SPF is
protection.

SPF stands for Sun Protection Factor. It is a measure used to describe how well a sunscreen protects against UVB-related sunburn when used as directed.

Broad-spectrum sunscreens are designed to protect against both UVA and UVB radiation.
