Medicine 101

Get introduced to healthcare, medical specialties, clinical thinking, and the world of medicine.

01 — Learn

Exploring medicine.

Medicine combines science, evidence, communication, and problem-solving to support human health.

01

Medical Specialties

Doctors can specialize in different areas, including dermatology, pediatrics, cardiology, surgery, and many others.

02

Clinical Thinking

Healthcare professionals use observations, patient information, scientific knowledge, and evidence to make decisions.

03

Evidence

Medical decisions can be informed by scientific research, clinical experience, and individual patient circumstances.

04

Communication

Clear communication is an important part of healthcare, from listening to patients to explaining information.
