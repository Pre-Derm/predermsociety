Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join Pre-Derm Society?

Students interested in dermatology, skincare science, medicine, research, or healthcare are welcome.

Do I need to want to become a dermatologist?

No. You can explore dermatology simply because you're interested in skin science and healthcare.

Start with our Skin 101 lessons and explore our student resources.
