ABOUT

Learn about
skin differently.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led educational initiative created to make dermatology and skin science easier to understand and explore.

Our mission is simple: make reliable skin education easier to access, encourage curiosity about dermatology, and help students share what they learn with their communities.
