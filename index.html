Our Mission

Making skin education feel simple.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led dermatology education community created to help students learn about skin health, skincare, and the world of dermatology.


Through educational lessons, guest speakers, resources, and community projects, we hope to inspire the next generation of students interested in dermatology.
