02

About Pre-Derm Society.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led organization created to make medicine, science, dermatology, and healthcare education easier for students to discover.

Our mission is to connect students with knowledge, educational resources, healthcare careers, research, and opportunities — especially when they don't know where to begin.

We want students to be able to go from "I'm interested in medicine" to "here's what I can actually explore."
