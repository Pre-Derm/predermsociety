About Pre-Derm

Making skin education feel approachable.

We believe everyone should have access to simple, trustworthy information about their skin. Pre-Derm Society creates educational lessons that introduce students to dermatology, skin health, prevention, and science.

“Our goal is to make learning about skin feel less intimidating and more accessible.”
