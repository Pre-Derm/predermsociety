02

Why Pre-Derm?

Pre-Derm Society exists to make medicine, science, and healthcare education easier to discover. We want students to be able to explore different medical careers, understand research, learn about skin health, and find opportunities without feeling like they have to figure everything out alone.

Our goal is to connect curiosity with action — giving students educational resources, reliable starting points, and pathways to explore their interests.
