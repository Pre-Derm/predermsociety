About Pre-Derm Society

Making skin science easier to understand.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led dermatology education initiative created to make learning about skin health, skincare, and dermatology accessible to students.


We create educational lessons, encourage students to explore dermatology, and work toward building meaningful educational opportunities within our community.

Our Purpose

Learn. Explore. Make an impact.

Pre-Derm Society is designed to give students a place to explore dermatology beyond the traditional classroom.


Our goal is to combine science education, community involvement, and student leadership to create meaningful opportunities for learning.
