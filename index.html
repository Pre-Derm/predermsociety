About us.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led organization created to make medicine, dermatology, science, and healthcare education easier for students to discover.

Our mission is to help students learn, explore their interests, discover opportunities, and feel more confident taking their first steps toward healthcare.

We want useful information to feel organized and accessible — whether someone is interested in dermatology, research, internships, or simply learning more about medicine.
