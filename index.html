ABOUT

Learn about
skin differently.

Pre-Derm Society is a student-led educational initiative created to make dermatology and skin science easier to understand and explore.

We believe knowledge becomes more meaningful when it is shared. Our mission is to make skin science accessible, encourage curiosity, and help students become educators in their own communities.

THE SKIN JOURNEY

Learn.

The Skin Journey is the heart of Pre-Derm Society. We believe learning about skin should not stop with the person who learns it. Knowledge can continue outward — from one student to another, from classrooms to communities.

01 · DISCOVER

Discover

Start by learning the foundations of skin science. Understand the structure and functions of the skin before exploring dermatology more deeply.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Understand

Explore topics such as acne science, the skin barrier, sun protection, ingredients, common skin conditions, and the science behind dermatology.

03 · TEACH

Teach

Turn knowledge into education. Student educators can use Pre-Derm Society resources to help make basic skin science easier for younger students and their communities.

04 · IMPACT

Impact

Track real educational outreach, lessons completed, lessons taught, student educators, community programs, and future partnerships.

OUR MISSION

Make skin science
easier to understand.

Pre-Derm Society exists to make dermatology education more accessible, understandable, and shareable — starting with students.

Through interactive lessons, educational resources, student-led teaching, and community outreach, we hope to create a growing journey of skin-science education.
