SKIN SCIENCE · LESSON 03

SUNSCREEN
101

Learn how ultraviolet radiation interacts with skin and why sun protection is an important part of skin health.

01 · THE FOUNDATION

Meet
UV radiation.

Ultraviolet radiation is a form of energy from the sun. Two types that are especially important to understand in everyday skin health are UVA and UVB.

UV exposure can contribute to sunburn, premature skin aging, and skin-cell damage. Sun protection helps reduce exposure.

02 · UVA & UVB

Two types.
Different effects.

UVA and UVB are both forms of ultraviolet radiation, but they interact with the skin differently. Understanding both helps explain why broad-spectrum protection matters.

01

UVA

UVA radiation can penetrate deeper into the skin and contributes to long-term skin changes and photoaging.

02

UVB

UVB radiation is strongly associated with sunburn and can contribute to damage in skin cells.

03

Broad-Spectrum

Broad-spectrum sunscreen is designed to provide protection against both UVA and UVB radiation.

03 · KEY IDEA

SPF is
not everything.

SPF primarily describes protection against UVB-related sunburn. A broad-spectrum sunscreen addresses both UVA and UVB radiation.

Sun protection is also about behavior: shade, protective clothing, hats, and avoiding intense midday sun can all help reduce UV exposure.
