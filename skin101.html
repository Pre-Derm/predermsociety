The Foundation

Meet your
skin.

Your skin is your body's largest organ. It protects you from the outside environment, helps regulate temperature, and allows you to experience touch.

Outer Layer

Epidermis

The epidermis is the outermost layer. It creates an important protective barrier between your body and the outside world.

Middle Layer

Dermis

The dermis sits beneath the epidermis. It contains blood vessels, nerves, glands, and hair follicles.

Deep Layer

Hypodermis

The hypodermis is the deepest layer. It contains connective tissue and helps cushion and insulate the body.
