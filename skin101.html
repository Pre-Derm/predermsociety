LESSON 01

Skin 101.

Your introduction to the science of skin.

🔎 What is skin?

Skin is the body's largest organ. It acts as a protective barrier between your body and the outside environment.

🧬 The layers of skin

Skin has three main layers: the epidermis, dermis, and subcutaneous tissue. Each layer has different jobs.

🛡️ Your skin barrier

The skin barrier is part of the outermost layer of your skin. It helps keep moisture in and helps protect your body from outside irritants.

💧 Hydration

Keeping skin hydrated supports the skin barrier. Moisturizers can help reduce water loss from the skin.

☀️ Sun protection

UV radiation can damage skin. Sun-protective habits are an important part of taking care of your skin.

🧴 Skincare basics

A simple routine can include gentle cleansing, moisturizing, and sun protection. More products are not always necessary.
