SKIN

A beginner-friendly introduction to the science of skin, its layers, and how it protects the body.

LESSON 01 · THE BASICS

Start with
the science.

Your skin is the body's largest organ. It does much more than simply cover the body — it protects, senses, regulates temperature, and helps keep the inside of your body stable.

01 · SKIN LAYERS

Three layers.
One system.

Skin is organized into three major layers. Each layer has a different structure and job.

01

Epidermis

The outermost layer of skin. It helps form a protective barrier between your body and the outside world.

02

Dermis

The layer underneath the epidermis. It contains blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles, and sweat glands.

03

Hypodermis

The deeper layer beneath the dermis. It contains connective tissue and helps cushion and insulate the body.

02 · WHAT SKIN DOES

More than
a barrier.

Skin helps protect the body from the environment, allows us to sense touch and temperature, helps regulate body temperature, and provides a physical barrier.

Understanding these basic functions is the foundation for understanding dermatology.
