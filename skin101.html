SKIN

Your introduction to the science of skin. Learn the basics, understand how your skin works, and build a foundation for exploring dermatology.

LESSON 01 · THE BASICS

Your skin
is an organ.

Your skin is the body's largest organ. It helps protect you from the outside environment, helps regulate temperature, and allows you to sense the world around you.

LESSON 02 · SKIN LAYERS

Three layers.
One system.

Skin is organized into three major layers. Each layer has a different structure and job.

01

Epidermis

The outermost layer. It forms a protective barrier between your body and the environment.

02

Dermis

The middle layer. It contains structures such as blood vessels, nerves, glands, and hair follicles.

03

Hypodermis

The deepest layer. It contains connective tissue and helps cushion and insulate the body.

LESSON 03 · WHAT SKIN DOES

More than
a barrier.

Skin performs several important jobs every day.

01

Protection

Skin helps protect the body from the environment.

02

Sensation

Nerves in the skin help us detect touch, pressure, and temperature.

03

Temperature

Blood flow and sweating help the body regulate temperature.

04

Barrier

The skin helps keep important substances inside the body and unwanted things outside.
