The science
of skin.

Skin is the body's largest organ. It acts as a protective barrier while helping regulate temperature, sense the environment, and protect the body from outside factors.

The outer layer

Epidermis

The epidermis is the outermost layer of skin. It helps form a protective barrier and contains cells involved in producing melanin.

The middle layer

Dermis

The dermis contains structures such as blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles, and sweat glands.

The deepest layer

Hypodermis

The hypodermis lies beneath the dermis and contains connective tissue and fat that help cushion and insulate the body.
