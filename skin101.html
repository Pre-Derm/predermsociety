Skin 101

Discover the basics of the body's largest organ and how its different structures work together.

01 — The Basics

What is skin?

01

The epidermis

The epidermis is the outermost layer of skin. It helps protect the body from the environment and contains cells involved in producing melanin.

02

The dermis

The dermis lies underneath the epidermis. It contains structures such as blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles and sweat glands.

03

The hypodermis

The hypodermis is the deeper layer beneath the dermis. It contains connective tissue and fat that help cushion and insulate the body.

04

The skin barrier

The outer skin barrier helps limit water loss and protects the body from outside irritants and microorganisms.
