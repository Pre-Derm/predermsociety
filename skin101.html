SKIN SCIENCE 01 · FOUNDATIONS

The science
of skin.

Let's start with the basics of skin structure and function.

01 · THE BASICS

What is skin?

Skin is the body's largest organ. It creates a protective boundary and also helps with sensation and temperature regulation.

02 · STRUCTURE

Three major layers.

Skin can be divided into three major regions.

01

Epidermis

The outermost layer of skin and an important part of the protective barrier.

02

Dermis

A deeper layer containing nerves, blood vessels, glands, and hair follicles.

03

Subcutaneous tissue

Deeper tissue that helps cushion and insulate the body.

03 · FUNCTION

More than a barrier.

Skin protects the body, allows us to sense our surroundings, and helps regulate temperature.
