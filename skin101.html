Skin Science 01 · Foundations

The science
of skin.

Before exploring dermatology, it helps to understand the structure and function of the skin.

01 · The basics

What is skin?

Skin is the body's largest organ. It forms a protective boundary between the body and its environment and also contributes to sensation and temperature regulation.

02 · Structure

Three major layers.

Skin is organized into three major regions. Each has different structures and functions.

01

Epidermis

The outermost layer of skin and an important part of the protective barrier.

02

Dermis

A deeper layer containing nerves, blood vessels, glands, and hair follicles.

03

Subcutaneous tissue

Deeper tissue containing connective tissue and fat that helps cushion and insulate the body.

03 · Function

More than a barrier.

Skin protects the body, allows us to sense our surroundings, and contributes to temperature regulation.
