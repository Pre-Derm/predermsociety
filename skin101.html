LESSON 01

Skin 101.

🔎 What is skin?

Skin is the body's largest organ. It protects us from the outside environment and helps regulate temperature and sensation.

🧬 Skin layers

The three main layers are the epidermis, dermis, and subcutaneous tissue.

🧴 The skin barrier

The skin barrier helps keep moisture in and protects the body from outside irritants.

☀️ Protect your skin

Sun protection is an important part of maintaining skin health throughout life.
