Skin 101

Your introduction to the science, structure, and function of the body's largest organ.

Start with
the basics.

Before exploring dermatology, it helps to understand the organ dermatologists study every day: the skin.

Your skin protects you, helps regulate temperature, allows you to sense your environment, and acts as a barrier between your body and the outside world.

The foundation

Three layers.
One complex organ.

Skin is organized into three major layers. Each layer has a different structure and purpose, but they work together to keep the body protected and functioning.

01

Epidermis

The outermost layer of skin. It forms a protective barrier and contains cells called keratinocytes.

02

Dermis

The layer underneath the epidermis. It contains blood vessels, nerves, glands, and hair follicles.

03

Hypodermis

The deepest layer. It contains connective tissue and fat and helps cushion and insulate the body.
