Lesson 01

Skin 101.

Your introduction to the science of skin. Learn what skin is made of, what it does, and why understanding it matters.

01 / The Basics

What is skin?

Your skin is the body's largest organ. It creates a protective barrier between your body and the outside environment.

Skin helps protect the body, regulate temperature, detect sensations, and maintain balance within the body.

Did You Know

Your skin is constantly renewing itself.

New skin cells are produced deeper within the epidermis and gradually move toward the surface.

02 / Structure

The three main layers.

Skin is organized into three major layers. Each layer has different structures and functions.

Layer 01

Epidermis

The outermost layer of the skin. It provides a protective barrier and contains cells called keratinocytes.

Layer 02

Dermis

The middle layer. It contains blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles, and glands.

Layer 03

Hypodermis

The deepest layer. It contains connective tissue and helps cushion and insulate the body.

03 / Why It Matters

Why should we care about skin health?

Healthy skin is about much more than appearance. Skin is an important part of the body's defense system.

Understanding basic skin science can help you evaluate skincare information and better understand common skin concerns.
