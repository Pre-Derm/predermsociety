SKIN SCIENCE · LESSON 01

SKIN
101

A simple introduction to the science of skin, created to make dermatology easier to understand, explore, and share.

01 · THE FOUNDATION

Start with
the skin.

Your skin is the body's largest organ. It protects what is underneath, helps regulate temperature, and allows you to sense the world around you.

Before learning about acne, treatments, or dermatology, it helps to understand how healthy skin is structured.

02 · SKIN SCIENCE

Three layers.
One system.

Skin is organized into three major layers. Each has a different job, but they work together to protect and support your body.

01

Epidermis

The outermost layer of the skin. It acts as a protective barrier and contains cells that help protect the body from the environment.

02

Dermis

The middle layer. It contains structures such as blood vessels, nerves, sweat glands, and hair follicles.

03

Hypodermis

The deepest layer. It contains connective tissue and fat that help cushion the body and provide insulation.

03 · KEY IDEA

Healthy skin
is a barrier.

One of the skin's most important jobs is creating a barrier between your body and the outside world. That barrier helps keep useful substances in while helping protect the body from environmental factors.
