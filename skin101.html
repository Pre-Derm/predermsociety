Lesson 01

The science
of skin.

Skin is much more than something we see. It is a complex organ that protects the body, helps regulate temperature, and allows us to sense the world around us.

Understanding the basic structure of skin is the first step toward understanding dermatology.

Outer layer

Epidermis

The outermost layer of skin. It helps create a protective barrier between your body and the outside environment.

Middle layer

Dermis

Contains structures such as blood vessels, nerves, glands, and hair follicles.

Deep layer

Hypodermis

The deepest layer contains connective tissue and helps cushion and insulate the body.

Healthy skin begins
with understanding.

Dermatology combines biology, medicine, and science to understand the skin and the conditions that can affect it.
