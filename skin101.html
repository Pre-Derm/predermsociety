LESSON 01 · DERMATOLOGY

Skin 101

Start with the foundation. Learn how the skin is structured, what each layer does, and why the skin is so important to the human body.

01 — THE FOUNDATION

Meet your skin.

Your skin is the body's largest organ. It helps protect your body from the outside environment, helps regulate temperature, allows you to sense your surroundings, and acts as an important protective barrier.

02 — SKIN STRUCTURE

Three layers.

The skin is commonly described as having three major layers. Each layer has different structures and functions, but they work together as one system.

01

Epidermis

The outermost layer of skin. It forms much of the body's protective barrier and contains cells involved in protection.

02

Dermis

The layer beneath the epidermis. It contains structures including blood vessels, nerves, sweat glands, and hair follicles.

03

Hypodermis

The deepest of these three layers. It contains connective tissue and fat that help cushion the body and provide insulation.

03 — KEY IDEA

Skin is
a barrier.

One of the skin's most important roles is to create a barrier between your body and the outside environment. The barrier helps protect the body while also helping maintain the body's internal environment.
