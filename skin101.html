Skin 101

Your introduction to the science of skin. Learn what your skin is made of, what each layer does, and why understanding your skin matters.

The Foundation

Your skin is
more complex
than it looks.

Skin is the body's largest organ. It helps protect the body, regulate temperature, sense the environment, and act as a barrier between you and the outside world.

Epidermis

The outermost layer of the skin. It provides a protective barrier and contains cells called keratinocytes.

Dermis

The layer beneath the epidermis. It contains blood vessels, nerves, glands, and structures that support the skin.

Hypodermis

The deepest layer beneath the dermis. It contains connective tissue and fat that help cushion and insulate the body.

Why It Matters

Skin science
starts here.

Understanding the basic structure of skin makes it easier to understand topics such as acne, sun protection, skin conditions, and skincare. Dermatology builds on these foundations.
