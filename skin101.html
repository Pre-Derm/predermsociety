Skin 101

Discover the basics of the body's largest organ and how its different structures work together.

01 — The Basics

Understanding your skin.

Skin is much more than an outer covering. It is a complex organ that protects the body, helps regulate temperature, and allows us to sense our environment.

01

The Epidermis

The epidermis is the outermost layer of skin. It provides much of the skin's protective barrier and contains cells involved in producing melanin.

02

The Dermis

The dermis lies beneath the epidermis. It contains blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles, sweat glands and other important structures.

03

The Hypodermis

The hypodermis is located underneath the dermis. It contains connective tissue and fat that help cushion and insulate the body.

04

The Skin Barrier

The skin barrier helps protect the body from the outside environment while also helping reduce water loss.
