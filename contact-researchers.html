Learn how to write a respectful, clear message when reaching out to researchers, universities, or programs.

01 · BEFORE YOU EMAIL

Be clear.
Be curious.

Research professionals receive many messages. A short, thoughtful email that explains who you are, why you are interested, and what you are asking can make your message easier to understand.

02 · YOUR MESSAGE
👋
01

Introduce Yourself

Briefly explain who you are and what you are interested in.

🔬
02

Show Interest

Mention a specific research topic or project that caught your attention.

❓
03

Ask Clearly

Ask one clear question about opportunities, advice, or learning more.

🙏
04

Be Respectful

Thank them for their time and understand that they may not be able to respond.
