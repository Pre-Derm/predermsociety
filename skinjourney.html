01 · OUR MISSION

Learn.
Understand.
Share.

The Skin Journey is an educational initiative created by Pre-Derm Society to make skin science easier to understand and more accessible to students.

We believe learning about skin should not be about perfection. It should be about understanding how the body's largest organ works and knowing where reliable information comes from.

03 · SKIN SCIENCE

Skin myths.
Meet science.

The internet is full of skincare advice. Learning how to recognize reliable information is an important part of your skin journey.

MYTH → FACT
"More skincare products automatically mean healthier skin."

FACT: Skin health is not determined by how many products someone uses. Different skin concerns can have different causes, and skincare should be based on individual needs and reliable health information.

MYTH → FACT
"Sun protection only matters on hot, sunny days."

FACT: Ultraviolet radiation can reach the skin even when the weather does not feel hot. Understanding UV exposure is an important part of learning about skin health.
