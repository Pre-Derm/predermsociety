SKIN SCIENCE · LESSON 02

ACNE

A simple introduction to acne, why it happens, and how understanding the skin can help us understand one of the most common skin conditions.

01 · THE FOUNDATION

Acne is
more than a breakout.

Acne is a common skin condition that develops when hair follicles become clogged with oil and dead skin cells.

Acne can appear in many forms and can affect people differently. Understanding what is happening inside the skin is the first step toward understanding acne.

02 · ACNE SCIENCE

What causes
acne?

Acne involves several factors working together. It is not simply caused by being "dirty."

01

Oil

Sebaceous glands produce sebum, an oily substance that helps keep skin from becoming excessively dry. Too much sebum can contribute to clogged pores.

02

Dead Skin

Dead skin cells are normally shed from the surface of the skin. When they build up with sebum, they can contribute to blocked follicles.

03

Bacteria

Certain bacteria naturally live on the skin. Changes inside clogged follicles can allow bacteria to contribute to inflammation.

04

Inflammation

The body's immune response can cause redness, swelling, and other visible signs of an inflamed acne lesion.

03 · KEY IDEA

Acne is
not a cleanliness test.

Acne is influenced by biology, hormones, genetics, oil production, follicular changes, bacteria, and inflammation. Having acne does not mean someone has poor hygiene.
