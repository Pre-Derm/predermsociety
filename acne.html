ACNE

Learn the basic science behind acne and why it is a common skin condition.

01 · THE BASICS

Understand
acne.

Acne is a common skin condition involving hair follicles and oil-producing glands. It can occur when follicles become clogged and inflammation develops.

02 · WHAT TO KNOW
🧴
01

Oil

Sebum is an oily substance produced by sebaceous glands in the skin.

🔬
02

Follicles

Acne develops in structures called pilosebaceous units, which include hair follicles and sebaceous glands.

🩹
03

Inflammation

Inflammation can contribute to some acne lesions.

🩺
04

Help

Persistent or severe acne can be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
