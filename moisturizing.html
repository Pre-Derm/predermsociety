Moisturizing

Explore hydration, moisturizers, and how these products support the skin barrier.

01 — Learn

Understanding moisturizers.

01

Why moisturize?

Moisturizers can help reduce dryness and support the skin's protective barrier.

02

Humectants

Humectants attract and hold water. Examples include ingredients such as glycerin and hyaluronic acid.

03

Emollients

Emollients help smooth the skin by filling spaces between skin cells.

04

Occlusives

Occlusives form a layer that helps reduce water loss from the skin's surface.
