LESSON 02

Moisturizing
matters.

💧 Why moisturize?

Moisturizers help support the skin barrier and reduce water loss from the outer layer of the skin.

🧬 The skin barrier

A healthy skin barrier helps maintain hydration and provides protection from environmental irritants.

✨ Different ingredients

Moisturizers can contain ingredients that attract water, soften the skin, or help reduce water loss.

🔎 Find what works

Skin is different for everyone. Learning how products work can help you make more informed skincare choices.
